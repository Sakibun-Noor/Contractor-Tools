**Template:** `01_Home_Page.png` — Active bottom tab: **Home**.

## Layout, top to bottom

1. **Header** — shared (see 00-README). Search placeholder: `Search Company, Product, Category, Trade or Keyword`.
2. **Hero banner** — full width, about 17% of the height. Left: three construction workers in hard hats
   and hi-vis vests looking at a tablet, with a city skyline and cranes. Center: "THE / CONSTRUCTION /
   TECHNOLOGY (orange) / —— DIRECTORY ——" and the tagline
   `CONSTRUCTION. TECHNOLOGY. CONTRACTORS. CONNECTED.` ("CONNECTED." in orange).
   Right: six hexagon icons (bar chart, robot arm, "AI" brain, cloud upload, building/BIM grid, shield with check).
3. **Heading line** — `CLICK ON ANY TO EXPLORE IT WORKFLOW SOLUTIONS`, bold navy caps, with an orange curved arrow on each side.
4. **Category grid** — 3 columns x 4 rows of white rounded cards, each an icon above a two-line label, in this order:

| | Col 1 | Col 2 | Col 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Row 1 | Project Management | AI & Automation | Safety & Compliance |
| Row 2 | Estimating & Takeoff | Fleet & Equipment | Finance & Payroll |
| Row 3 | Procurement | BIM & Documents | Field Operations |
| Row 4 | CRM & Sales | Leads & Bids | Marketing & Reputation |

   Icons (existing category art): calendar with check, AI chip, shield with check, blueprint with cube,
   truck with pin, calculator with dollar, cart with box, document, map pin, people with check,
   clipboard with magnifier, megaphone.
5. **Info strip** — navy band, four columns separated by thin dividers:
   - `FIND` (blue, magnifier) — "Locate construction technology providers."
   - `LEARN` (orange, graduation cap) — "Understand software before you buy."
   - `RESEARCH` (green, building with check) — "Research trusted technology providers."
   - `INSIGHTS` (yellow, light bulb) — "Stay informed about construction technology."
6. **Bottom navigation** — shared.

## Behavior (from the template and existing site)

- Each category card goes to the Search / Results page filtered to that category (same slugs as landscape).
- The search field offers autosuggest (same module as landscape).
- The hamburger opens a menu (contents not shown in any template).

## Template notes

- Category order differs from landscape (landscape: Finance, CRM, Leads, Estimating, Project Management, Field Ops /
  Safety, Fleet, Marketing, AI, BIM, Procurement). The template order above is the one to use on portrait.
- The heading wording `CLICK ON ANY TO EXPLORE IT WORKFLOW SOLUTIONS` differs from the live site
  ("Click on any category to explore it workflow solutions"). Use the template wording on portrait.
- The info strip and the bottom navigation both sit at the bottom of the first screen and use a lot of height.
- Footer is not shown in the template.
